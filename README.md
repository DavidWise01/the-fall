The area between the curves and the horizon each takes to re-cross a recovery threshold are computed live (verified: with-plan ~11,700 yr vs without ~28,500 yr &mdash; the trough compressed, not the fall). AMBER: the &lsquo;30,000 &rarr; 1,000 years&rsquo; is Asimov&rsquo;s premise and both curves are stylised, not a forecast; what is honest is the shape of the claim &mdash; intervention that changes a recovery RATE changes the DURATION of the trough far more than its depth.
